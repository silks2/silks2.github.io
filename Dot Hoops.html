Dot Hoops

WASD move · click a teammate to pass · hold SPACE to charge a shot and release at the right moment (the bar has no sweet spot marked, so judge it by distance: farther shots need more power, but a completely full bar is an airball) · Shift / E to swipe for a steal when defending (risky, it can be a foul) · right click is a skill: with the ball it sidesteps the way you are moving (fails if you dash into a defender); without it, it tackles the carrier only when you and they are walking into each other.
Without the ball, click one of your dots to take control of it.
Mobile: left stick moves, tap a dot to pass or switch, hold SHOOT then release, SKILL sidesteps or tackles.
4 quarters, 24 second shot clock, 3 pointers, out of bounds, fouls, overtime.
